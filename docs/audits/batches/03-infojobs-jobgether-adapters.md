### Design decision for change phase
Capture is **site-specific**, but after observed canonical identity and matched detail, pass a common normalized record to the existing JOLT validation/provenance intake. Do not loosen identity/hash checks to conceal failures. Add privacy-safe examples of *actual* browser cards and detail pages to tests before changing layouts.

The eight paths above are audited for static source behavior; their Windows live execution is **unverified**, and any observed success on unit fixtures is not acceptance.
